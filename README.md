## 🚀 Featured Project

### 💧 AquaSense AI

**Intelligent Campus Water Management & Demand Prediction System**

A full-stack system designed to monitor campus water usage, detect anomalies, predict demand, and support water allocation planning.

**Tech Stack:**
`ESP32` • `FastAPI` • `SQLite` • `React` • `TypeScript` • `Tailwind CSS` • `Python` • `Scikit-learn`

**Key Features:**

- 📡 Real-time water-flow telemetry using ESP32
- 💧 Dual flow-sensor monitoring
- 📊 Water usage analytics
- 🚨 Rule-based anomaly & water-loss detection
- 📈 Explainable demand prediction
- 🧠 Machine-learning based analytics
- 💦 Water supply allocation planning

## 🔬 Research & Development

### 🍎 Portable Multispectral Pesticide Screening Device

Currently working on a research-oriented prototype for **non-destructive pesticide screening in fresh produce**.

The system combines **multispectral sensing, controlled UV/visible/IR illumination, imaging, and edge-AI processing** to explore pesticide-level classification.

**Target Classification:**

`PURE` → `LOW` → `HIGH`

**Core Technologies & Hardware:**

- Raspberry Pi 5
- Pi Camera Module 3
- AS7265x multispectral sensor
- UV / White / IR illumination
- Edge-AI processing
- Optical sensing chamber
- Load-cell based measurement
- Environmental sensing
